## Brief and result

The author's request was another pass to reduce LLM writing, particularly isolated one-liners. This pass works on paragraph rhythm. It keeps the accepted chapter order, arguments, historical episodes, examples and research evidence. Chapters 6–12 remain WIP. Chapter 13 is unchanged byte-for-byte, as are the preface and Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 5.

The recurring problem was an example followed by several isolated explanations of what it meant. Ordinary transitions and repeated conclusions were receiving the same visual emphasis as discoveries and jokes. Most edits join those sentences to the paragraph they complete or introduce. A smaller number remove redundant commentary or adjust the sentence connection. No new anecdotes or research claims were added.

## Decisions

- **Chapter 3:** retain the complete history through coding models, copy and paste, context, tools, persistent instructions, separate histories and app builders. Preserve the harness definition, five-layer map and the Merge Sort and Count-Min Sketch investigations. Fold explanatory asides and paired takeaway sentences into paragraphs.
- **Chapter 6:** keep the editing failures and the pattern card. Bring the philosophical questions and their consequences closer together. The distinctions between acceptance and pursuit, storage and retrieval, and confidence and contact remain.
- **Chapter 7:** reduce the succession of short teacher/rung payoffs. Keep Omar, the historical survey, Good's unexploded “better,” the shadow history and the constitutional argument. Remove one redundant compiler/research recursion summary after the mechanism has been explained.
- **Chapter 8:** integrate transitions with the supervisory problem or instrument they introduce. Keep the interpretability caveats beside the methods. All numerical research claims remain unchanged.
- **Chapter 9:** connect the questions about learning and decision support into sustained paragraphs. Preserve the distinction between clarification, preference discovery and transformation, and keep the jokes that expose what can go wrong.
- **Chapter 10:** reduce the succession of slogans about interfaces, control and institutions. Preserve the distinction between the imagined fluent interaction and the actual work of editing the book.
- **Chapter 11:** keep Mei, Sami and Lea in view while folding the commentary around them. Remove a redundant warning before the customer/phone-call example and a few repeated declarations of the architecture's lesson. The first experiment remains prospective.
- **Chapter 12:** join staged takeaways about capacity, learning and meaning. Preserve the ideology vortex, Derrida provocation, conflicting goods, portability and shared-room argument. The two questions about resources and a worthwhile life now sit together, without a paragraph containing only “and.”

## Rereading decisions

Joining paragraphs is not automatically an improvement. The reread restored the break where Chapter 10 turns from the editing record to the author's continuing labor, retained a separate paragraph for Bloom's tutoring evidence, and preserved the final octopus line in Chapter 12 as a landing. The teacher-in-the-walls sentence was attached to the preceding explanation rather than to the new backgammon example. Several shortened transitions were folded again so the edit would not simply produce a fresh set of isolated slogans.

Short lines retained deliberately include “No bars,” “Congratulations. Infinite self-improvement,” “Even the X-ray needs a radiologist,” “Very efficient. / Slightly evil,” and the children/dinner passage. The pattern card and useful displayed definitions remain readable as artifacts.

# System 3 — Visual Art Direction

This directory is the source of truth for the book's illustration language, especially chapter opener pages.

The goal is not visual consistency for its own sake. The goal is to make every chapter opener feel like a piece of the same authored book while giving each chapter a visual idea that could only belong to that chapter.

## The benchmark

The visual benchmark is the current Chapter 11 artwork:

`resources/chapter-openers/chapter-11-capacity-over-power.jpg`

The *Capacity Over Power* page establishes the quality bar for:

- **2:3 portrait chapter-page proportions**.
- Warm, lightly textured **cream paper**.
- Restrained watercolor with fine ink drawing.
- Muted **Prussian / denim blue, ochre, warm gray, olive, and parchment**.
- Hand-lettered chapter number in blue, large title in black, subtitle in blue.
- Generous negative space and a clear visual hierarchy.
- One dominant emotional / philosophical idea rather than a visual summary of the chapter.
- Literary illustration, not slideware, advertising, fantasy concept art, or glossy AI imagery.

Chapter 11 is the **aesthetic benchmark**, not a command that every chapter must contain only one object. Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 show why some chapters need richer editorial structure.

## Three benchmark modes

### 1. Chapter 11 — aesthetic benchmark

Use for color, paper, proportions, typography, restraint, breathing room, and emotional clarity.

The image works because it does not explain *Capacity Over Power*. The open doors *embody* it.

### 2. Chapter 3 — architecture with soul

Deep Mode is allowed to show the actual architecture because the architecture itself is the chapter's intellectual invention. The successful direction is a human/orchestrator at the center with research, building, criticism, and visual thinking happening around them.

The lesson: **specific complexity is better than generic minimalism**. Strip labels and clutter, not the intellectual content.

### 3. Chapter 4 — editorial / evidence page

System 3 deliberately breaks the pure-watercolor grammar by inserting an actual photographic evidence object into the page. The reader participates in the chapter before reading it by judging seven claims.

The lesson: **the artifact can be part of the argument**. A taped photograph, handwritten note, or paper insert is welcome when it is doing intellectual work.

## Non-negotiables

### Format

- Every chapter opener is one **standalone 2:3 portrait page**.
- Never generate a grid, contact sheet, collage of multiple chapter covers, or landscape composition as the final opener.
- The title area normally occupies the upper 20–30% of the page; the illustration owns the rest.
- Preserve generous margins and paper around the art.

### Typography

Default hierarchy:

1. `CHAPTER N` — small uppercase blue hand lettering, usually underlined.
2. Chapter title — large expressive black brush / ink lettering.
3. Subtitle — medium blue handwritten lettering, optionally with one short underline.

Avoid mixing in polished serif display type, tech fonts, corporate sans serif, or digital UI typography.

Do not add explanatory prose simply because there is empty space.

### Palette and rendering

- Cream / warm off-white paper.
- Desaturated blue watercolor washes.
- Warm ochre / sunlight.
- Olive / sage foliage.
- Charcoal / dark blue-gray ink contours.
- Limited accents; avoid saturated neon.
- Visible watercolor blooms, imperfect ink, paper grain, soft edges.
- The rendering should feel handmade and editorial, not photoreal fantasy art or 3D animation.

### Composition

A strong opener should be understandable at thumbnail scale.

Prefer:

- one dominant metaphor or scene;
- one clear focal point;
- small secondary details that reward rereading;
- asymmetry and breathing room;
- depth created by landscape, architecture, or foreground / background relationships.

Avoid:

- dashboards;
- bullet lists;
- diagrams with many arrows;
- labeled role boxes;
- fake UI cards;
- floating icons;
- crowded crowds of identical robots;
- explanatory signs;
- generic “AI future” imagery;
- sci-fi glow, cyberpunk, metallic blue light, or fantasy crystals.

### Intellectual specificity

Before illustrating a chapter, ask:

> What is the one visual idea that becomes richer *after* reading this chapter?

The opener should not merely match the chapter title. It should capture the chapter's conceptual turn.

Examples:

- Chapter 1: releasing control allows emergence.
- Chapter 3: an inquiry can choose what kind of cognition it needs next.
- Chapter 4: claims arrive with different epistemic status.
- Chapter 5: individual knowers become an institution capable of accumulating and challenging knowledge.
- Chapter 8 / Layer 4: the AI must not own the choice of which road is worth taking.
- Chapter 11: more capacity can open more room for human life.

## Recurring visual language

### Robots

When robots appear, they are recurring storybook agents, not product mascots:

- small to human-scale;
- slightly worn, mechanical, hand-built;
- cream / brass / muted gray-blue bodies;
- simple expressive dark faces / eyes;
- physically engaged in work: reading, observing, building, recording, testing, arguing.

Do not fill a page with dozens of identical robots. A society is shown through **different positions and activities**, not robot count.

### Humans

Humans should feel ordinary, curious, and embodied rather than heroic.

- practical clothing;
- seen from behind or in three-quarter view when identity is not the point;
- gestures matter more than facial drama;
- humans belong inside the epistemic network, not above it as omniscient gods.

### Architecture

Architecture can carry memory and institutional meaning.

Old stone, observatories, libraries, workshops, doors, bridges, terraces, and towers work well because they imply structures that predate the current actor and can outlive them.

Use architecture symbolically, but never label the symbolism on the page.

## Visual narrative across the book

The openers should quietly form their own arc:

1. **Release** — capability is allowed to emerge.
2. **Search** — the search procedure itself becomes searchable.
3. **Inquiry** — cognition becomes a repertoire of possible moves.
4. **Evidence** — reality interrupts fluent language.
5. **Institution** — knowledge becomes social and structured.
6. **Inheritance** — institutions develop culture and reusable patterns.
7. **Sparse human signal** — human attention guides much larger machine search.
8. **Recursion** — the machinery of improvement becomes editable.
9. **Desire** — the objective itself cannot simply be delegated.
10. **Fluency** — complex inquiry disappears underneath a simple intention.
11. **Practice** — the architecture encounters the real world of a store.
12. **Capacity** — the question becomes what more humans can become.
13. **Dream** — *The Prophecy* breaks into absurd, philosophical fiction.

The illustrations should therefore become richer in meaning as the book progresses without becoming more visually crowded.

## Editorial note: Chapter 11 title

The current manuscript chapter is titled **After Capacity — A Glimpse of Double Descent Life**, while the benchmark artwork is titled **Capacity Over Power — What Becomes of Humans**.

Do not silently resolve this mismatch in artwork. It remains an editorial decision. For visual-direction purposes, the existing *Capacity Over Power* artwork remains the quality benchmark regardless of the final title.

## SOLID drafting decisions worth reusing

- Opened on the book he'd just read (*SOLID is not Solid*, David Bryant Copeland), confirmed real by search; the widget-controller example taken from Copeland's own post so the break was faithful.
- "A small theology of clean code" echoed the earlier SOLID piece on purpose; flag echoes and let him choose.
- Two closers stacked blunt each other ("mission accomplished" + "hello fellow zombies"): cut one.
- "Start over" could read as "scrap this essay": "Time to start over" fixes the ambiguity.
- "Fellow zombies" has a small thematic friction (zombies are mindless; the narrator just woke up). Named, offered alternatives, recommended keeping it for the train-ride register. He decides.
- The de-LLM pass stripped "not X — Y" firing every few lines, rule-of-three lists and same-fragment paragraph endings, and let some sentences run long and unpolished.

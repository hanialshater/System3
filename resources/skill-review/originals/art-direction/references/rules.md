# Rules and worked example

## Scorecard (per chapter or proof)

| Dimension | Asks |
|---|---|
| Style consistency | One hand across the pages? |
| Argument-carrying | Do images draw sentences, and do the needed diagrams exist? |
| Text–image fit | Literal pairings vs stock/filler, per chapter |
| Layout | Narrow wrapped columns, bleed into margins, text ending mid-paragraph above a picture |
| Completeness | Missing sections, missing diagrams, stale subtitles, stray badges |
| **As a proof** / **As art** | Scored separately (the 50-page proof: 6.5 as a proof, 8 as art) |

## Robot bible

One manufacturing language: brass / cream / blue material family; the same joint logic; the same indicator-light language; the same age and patina; two or three sanctioned head families. Specialists vary by function. The mechanical herons may differ but must look built by the same civilisation as the Ch 6 robot. Accidental variety (round brass, CRT-screen smiling, black-faced service, swarm, birds) reads as separate generations, not worldbuilding.

## Typography

Generate openers with an empty title zone; set CHAPTER X / TITLE / SUBTITLE in the layout. Generated lettering drifts in kerning, anatomy, punctuation and spelling across a printed book. If a chapter title changes, the opener's lettering must be reviewed against `curated/provenance.json` before reuse.

## Worked example: Chapter 5 in the illustrated proof

**Keep (12):** the cover; the Earth with the green light (repaint the photoreal globe in house style); clay vessel and tokens; the reviews warehouse; the theatre with the nurse; the checklist and pilot's hand; Millikan's apparatus; the five coats; the camera obscura; the sea-floor stripes; the loose connector; the glass box.

**Replace (5):**
- Qin: grain sacks and a bowl miss it. The sentence is "You can hold one and read the decision" — draw the bronze measure with the edict cast into its side.
- The bureaucracy queue: stock. The tablet-and-clerk scene, or nothing.
- "We call it science": scientists, a robot and books labelled INSTRUMENTS, DATA, CRITICISM, COMMUNITIES — and labelled books again on the next page.
- Senku and Kaseki: keep the boy generic; but the glassblowing is fan service while the potter and apprentice, the actual argument, have no picture.
- "Sixteen Claudes, Again": a busy workbench saying nothing the cover didn't. The eight-verb frame goes here.

**Add (3):** the chapter opener (queue at the `linux/` door, one robot reading `progress.md`); the harness ladder of failures and fixes; the seven-plus-one frame, with its seven panels matching the seven section headings and the eighth arrow returning to rearrange the frame.

## Print

300 PPI at placed size; originals and enhanced derivatives kept separately with provenance (enhanced pixels are not captured detail); lossless embedding; no arbitrary CMYK conversion of RGB masters; bleed, binding, output profile and a physical proof wait for the printer.

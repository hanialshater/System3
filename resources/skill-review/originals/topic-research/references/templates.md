## Research base (mode B) — the repo format

```
# Chapter N Research Base: <topic>

## Chapter boundary
What the previous chapter ends on. What this chapter must answer. What the next chapter owns
(do not solve it here). Where this chapter should stop.

## Core thesis
One or two sentences, provisional.

## 1. <Strand>
### <Source or thinker> (year)
- What it shows, in two or three lines.
- The operation in this book it could demonstrate.
- Scene potential: is there a person, a failure, a moment?
- Primary source; status (verified / reported / to check).

## Candidate exhibits, ranked
## Author's own material to look for (questions for story-mining)
## Collisions with other chapters
## Shelf life: what will date first
```

Rule carried from Chapter 8: a research base lists far more than a chapter can hold. Pick the few that demonstrate operations, and give at least one of them an author-side scene, or the chapter becomes a catalogue.

# Voice

## What his Arabic does

- Tells a story and undercuts it in the same breath: «بس خلينا نرجع خطوة لورا.» The self-undermining move is instinctive in his Arabic; in the English book it had to be constructed (The Crack).
- Philosophy from the street: a parking spot leads to game theory and then to Ostrom, with Uncle Jalal in the same paragraph.
- Funny without announcing it. Humour lives in the gap between what is said and meant: «ممتاز يا حج، هات المعادلة.»
- Code-switches English technical terms mid-sentence, the way engineers talk: «وبعدين تفتح VS Code», «سمّاها implementation detail».

## Markers to carry through the body of sentences

Levantine connective tissue: إشي، هيك، كتير، زلمة، بدنا / بدي / بدك، عشان، هون، شو، ليش، لسا، حدا، رح، مش، هاي / هاد، اللي، بس، خلّينا، يعني، طيب، كمان، قدّيش، منيح، مزبوط، هات.

Corrections seen in his drafts: «بيدكشف / ما بندكشف» → بنكتشف؛ «ما حد» → ما حدا؛ «بقدرنا» → منقدر (recorded as the correction at the time; but see below, «منقدر» is the Syrian/Lebanese form and Jordanian usually says «بنقدر». Ask him which he wants before using either.)

MSA connectors that betray a Levantine text when they aren't deliberate: ليست، لكنّ، إنّما، بل، الذي/التي، سوف، لم/لن، هذا/هذه in running speech. «إنه» is *not* one of them: it is the ordinary Levantine «that» («لتجربة إنه حدا يطنّشك»). A deliberate formal phrase inside colloquial speech is a joke and is fine: «تم الأخذ بالملاحظة».

## Jordanian, not just Levantine

Hani is Jordanian; how he writes it (which city's forms, how much Syrian/Lebanese he tolerates) is his to say, so confirm against his own Arabic when you have it (`author-arabic-sample.md`). Until then, lean to common Jordanian forms and away from the Damascus/Beirut ones a model drifts to:

| Prefer (Jordanian) | Over (Syrian/Lebanese) |
|---|---|
| بحكي / قاعد بحكي (progressive) | عم بحكي |
| بنقدر، بنعرف، بنشوف (b- for "we") | منقدر، منعرف، منشوف |
| هسّا / هسّع | هلّق / هلأ |
| عشان | منشان، كرمال |
| هاد / هاي | هيدا / هيدي |
| إنه | إنو |
| مش | مو |

Spelling in running text: إنه (not انو)، اللي، مش. English terms take الـ attached (الـevaluator، الـprompts). Write ق as ق whatever the pronunciation. `ar_register.py --list nonjo` lists the right-hand column in a draft.

## House devices

| English | Arabic | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Therefore:** (Alexander's pattern lines) | **عشان هيك:** | keeps the rhythm |
| confidence asterisks | ★★ / ★ | can't be mistaken for a formatting error |
| "the fold" | الطيّة، تعبير ورثته المواقع عن الصحف | localised gloss; «أعلى الصفحة الأولى» works without one |
| pipes (as image, older draft) | مواسير | Levantine wink beats أنابيب |
| "seven jobs and an eighth" (Ch 5; an older draft said "eight verbs") | نتذكّر، نوحّد المقاييس، نتخصّص، نختلف كلٌّ من طريقه، نرصد، نتتبّع المصدر، نوزّع الانتباه، نراجع المؤسسة نفسها | first-person plural beats gerunds; bare «نوحّد» echoes التوحيد and «نختلف باستقلال» is a calque |
| subtitle for Ch 6 | لما صارت المعرفة software | echoes the blog series title |

## Cases

**Aphorisms explained instead of translated** (System 3 Ch 5, first pass; some of these English lines have since changed, as of 2026-10-03. Re-check with `grep -rn "<line>" /home/user/System3/chapters` before citing one as current):
- "Remembering is not knowing" ~~الذاكرة وحدها لا تعرف الصواب~~ → التذكّر ليس معرفة
- "Knowledge becomes structure" ~~تأخذ المعرفة شكلًا يستطيع الناس العمل داخله~~ → تتحوّل المعرفة إلى بنية
- "The organization was the bug" ~~الخلل هذه المرة كان في المؤسسة نفسها~~ → book: كانت المؤسسة نفسها هي الـbug؛ blog: طلعت المؤسسة هي الـbug. («العلّة» was the earlier choice; "bug" is the engineer joke after the compiler story, so keep it unless Hani wants العلّة.)
- "Rigor at scale is not the elimination of trust. It is the organization of trust." → حين يتّسع العلم، تكون الصرامة في تنظيم الثقة، لا في إلغائها (the translator's version beat the suggested one)

**Stress and grammar** (Ch 5 draft; the line has since changed): "Who knows what matters. Who sees what matters too." ~~يهمّ من يعرف ماذا~~ (sentence-final ماذا is un-Arabic) → المهم من يعرف. والمهم أيضًا من يرى.

**Engineer jokes stay engineer jokes:** "Reality has a commit hook" → صار للواقع commit hook, not صار للواقع صوت يعترض عند تسجيل التعديلات. His readers are engineers.

**Small upgrades a good translator made:** «وفي ثناياها نفسية شخص» → «وتلمح فيها شخصًا» (نفسية was the one clinical word in a warm paragraph); «تعاون آخر» → «فريق بحثي آخر».

**Localisations that earn their place** (from earlier Ch 5 drafts; the Chen example is gone from the current English): «لم يكن مكيال تشين هو المكيال الذي اختاره الكون»; «قسم فلسفة تصله فاتورة حوسبة مفزعة»; «شاهد واحد يبدّل معطفه»; Ibn al-Haytham reading as a homecoming rather than an import.

**Engaging over faithful (Ch 6, Levantine), each reported to Hani:** «مش ابن عمها المريح» for Fermat; «عملنا تماس مع الواقع، وكسبنا اجتماع»; «مبروك: عملنا parallelization لتجربة إنه حدا يطنّشك».

**Translator embellishment to flag, not keep silently:** «أو أننا فقط متفقون على إهانة الخشب» (The Dream preface) was charming and not his.

**Trailer problem (The Dream Ch 1):** the opening spent «القبو مولّع» and the castle image before their home chapters. Hold killer lines for where they land; a trailer shows the explosion, not the twist.

**Two openings competing:** «هاي قصة / مش أي قصة» and «هدفي في هذا الكتاب… بنّاء فيلسوف» stacked; earn the mission with the story first.

**Op-ed MSA (Al Jazeera, "Welcome to the Greatest Hallucination"):** first version read as translated. The fix was rewriting sections from Arabic thinking rather than repairing sentences. Renderings: الهلوسة الكبرى (echoes political terminology); simulacrum → المحاكاة المطلقة; LinkedIn and IKEA references adapted for an Arab readership; economic critique tied to Gulf AI investment.

## The reverse direction

"Write the English with the Arabic brain": before revising an English chapter, a one-paragraph ammiya version of its core argument reminds him what his voice sounds like when he isn't performing English-language nonfiction.

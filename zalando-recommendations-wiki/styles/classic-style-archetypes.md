# Classic style archetypes (Kibbe / McJimsey)

**evidence: sourced**

Harriet McJimsey identified six style archetypes in the 1960s — Dramatic, Natural, Gamine, Classic, Ingenue, Romantic — that most modern style quizzes still trace back to. David Kibbe's 1987 system built on this: five core "essences" (Dramatic, Natural, Classic, Gamine, Romantic) plus eight hybrid subtypes, classified by the balance of soft/curved ("Yin") vs. sharp/angular ("Yang") lines in body and face — not size or weight. Still in active use by image consultants.

Sources: https://theconceptwardrobe.com/kibbe-body-types/the-13-kibbe-body-types-their-clothing-lines · https://robertastylelee.co.uk/what-is-a-style-personality/

| Archetype | Defining traits |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Clean lines, symmetry, tailored fits, neutral/muted palette, minimal ornamentation. Favors investment pieces over trend-chasing. |
| **Romantic** | Soft/curved seams, chiffon/lace/velvet, florals, ruffles, fitted-but-flowing silhouettes. |
| **Dramatic** | Sharp vertical lines, architectural silhouettes, high-contrast/monochrome, statement accessories. |
| **Natural** | Loose/unconstructed fits, natural fibers, earthy tones, comfort and ease of movement prioritized. |
| **Gamine** | Mixed masculine/feminine, mixed delicate/bold in one outfit, graphic contrast, playful/trend-mixing. |

## Why this layer is useful

This is the **stable** half of a style fingerprint — closer to a durable characteristic than a situational one (see the durable-vs-situational caution in [../GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md)'s persona entry, which applies here too). Once inferred with reasonable confidence, it should persist across sessions rather than being re-derived each time.

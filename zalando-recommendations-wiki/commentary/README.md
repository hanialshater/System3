## The two rules that matter most if you only read two

1. A false or unsupported claim is worse than no claim. When in doubt, say less.
2. Every sentence should be doing exactly one job — explaining or persuading, never both. See [explain-vs-persuade.md](explain-vs-persuade.md).

## One rule about *when* to explain, not just what to say

Surfacing "why" reasoning at non-critical decision points can reduce trust and slow decisions without raising perceived usefulness — one study found this held even when the explanation itself was accurate. `evidence: sourced` — https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2023.1151150/full. Reserve detailed rationale for actual decision moments (hovering a size, near Add to Bag) — don't attach an explanation to every single item card.

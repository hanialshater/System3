# Voice: help-seeker, not help-provider

**evidence: sourced**

## The rule

Write commentary in first person, offering a best guess ("I picked this because…") rather than in third-person, authoritative-directive style ("This is a great match for you because…").

## Why

A controlled study comparing explanation phrasing found "help-seeker" framing earned more user trust than "help-provider" framing — offering a best guess reads as more honest than asserting a confident, unqualified claim.

Source: https://medium.com/@ekinceylan/explainable-recommendation-building-trust-for-algorithms-63ea7288a011

## Pair it with a correction affordance

Scrutable systems — ones that let the customer see and correct the reasoning behind a recommendation — build more durable trust than a one-way justification, because the explanation becomes a feedback loop instead of just a claim. Where the surface allows it, pair a piece of commentary with a lightweight correction ("not quite my style" / edit-preference), not just a static rationale.

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.19302

## Examples

- **Do:** "I picked this because you've been comparing two trail shoes on cushioning — here's how they differ."
- **Don't:** "This is the perfect match for your active lifestyle."

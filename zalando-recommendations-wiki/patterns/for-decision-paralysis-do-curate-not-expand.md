# For decision paralysis, do curate down — never expand the assortment further

**evidence: sourced**

## Trigger

A category or filtered page with a large number of near-identical options, and behavior suggesting overwhelm rather than exploration: fast scrolling with no opens, filters applied then abandoned, or a session that ends without a decision despite many items viewed.

## Do

Narrow, don't widen. Surface a smaller, well-differentiated set rather than more of the same, and make the differentiation itself visible (what's actually different between these five options, not just that there are five of them). If the underlying issue is filter/navigation usability rather than the assortment itself, fix that first — see the "why" below.

## Why

Baymard's product-list research found sites with weak filtering see 67-90% abandonment among users seeking a specific product type, vs. 17-33% on sites with strong filter/sort tooling; 38% of sites lack filters for attributes they actually display, and 54% over-categorize (treating an attribute like color as a top-level category instead of a filter). `evidence: sourced` — https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists. Classic choice-overload research (the Iyengar/Lepper jam study, replicated in retail) found a 24-item display drew more browsers but converted at 3% vs. 30% for a 6-item display; a large-scale study of 1.6M consumers attributed 64% of purchase-probability decline to shoppers avoiding comparison entirely when facing too many similar options. `evidence: sourced` — https://www.crobox.com/blog/choice-overload

## Don't

- Don't respond to "customer isn't converting on this page" by adding more recommendation modules — that's very often the opposite of what the page needs. A bounded test should always be willing to lose against simplification (see [../process/evaluation-rubric.md](../process/evaluation-rubric.md)).
- Don't confuse decision paralysis with comparison friction — paralysis is *too many* options with no way to narrow; comparison friction is a *specific small set* the customer can't resolve between. See [for-comparison-friction-do-comparison-rx.md](for-comparison-friction-do-comparison-rx.md) for the latter.

# Trust and quality uncertainty

**evidence: sourced**

## The evidence

- Word-of-mouth (89% high/medium trust) and reviews/ratings (85%) carry 2-3x the trust of brand advertising or brand websites. Shoppers hitting a negative review often keep researching (nearly half) or cross-check other reviewers (over a third); a higher-rated, more-reviewed product beats a cheaper alternative in preference. — https://www.bazaarvoice.com/blog/why-customer-testimonials-and-peer-reviews-are-key-to-shopper-trust-in-2025/
- Because shoppers can't physically handle fabric or check construction online, they use **price/discount depth as a quality proxy** when a more direct signal is unavailable — a worse substitute for the real trust job. — https://fashionandtextiles.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s40691-018-0128-2

## Signals that suggest this need

First-time exposure to a brand (no purchase or browse history with it), long dwell time on a product page without add-to-bag, repeat visits without progress, Partner Program / marketplace item (see [../categories/README.md](../categories/README.md)) rather than first-party or established private-label.

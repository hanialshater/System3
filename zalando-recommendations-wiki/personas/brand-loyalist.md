# Brand loyalist

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

Strong preference for specific brands, reduced price sensitivity within those brands, high repeat-purchase rate, tendency to become a brand advocate. Values consistency and being treated well over discount-seeking — the clear behavioral counterpoint to [deal-hunter.md](deal-hunter.md).

Source: https://1worldsync.com/resource-center/blog/shopper-persona-the-brand-loyalist/

## Commentary implications

- Return-hesitation and trust needs are typically *low* here — trust is already established. Don't over-hedge fit or quality claims for a brand this customer has bought from repeatedly.
- The highest-leverage move is referencing specific traceable history, not generic style talk — see [../patterns/for-loyal-customer-do-reference-history.md](../patterns/for-loyal-customer-do-reference-history.md).
- Don't push discount/deal framing at this persona — it's the wrong lever and may read as devaluing the relationship (contrast with [deal-hunter.md](deal-hunter.md)).

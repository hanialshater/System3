# Zalando's Z-Types

**evidence: sourced**

Zalando Media Solutions mined purchase-pattern data from 22 million active customers (combined with anonymized behavioral data) into seven named behavioral archetypes:

- **Happy Casuals**
- **Fresh Families** — maps closely to [occasion-gift-and-family-shopper.md](occasion-gift-and-family-shopper.md)
- **Modern Mainstreamers**
- **Hip Poppers** — teens/young adults who favor peer-group fit over standing out
- **Street Snobs** — maps toward [trend-seeker.md](trend-seeker.md)
- **Cultured Elite** — maps toward [luxury-aspirational.md](luxury-aspirational.md)
- **Preppy Strivers**

Zalando's own investor/company communications describe Z-Types as segmenting by *observed shopping behavior*, not demographics, specifically so marketing and creative can be tailored per segment.

Sources: https://t3n.de/news/zalando-ztype-typologie-kunden-kaeufer-personas-915313/ · https://geschaeftsbericht.zalando.de/2017/magazin/mission-ztypes-das-passende-marketing-fuer-jede-zielgruppe/

## Why this matters

This is the single most directly relevant artifact in this wiki, because it's Zalando's own evidence that behavior-derived segments — not raw demographics — are the right unit here. Treat the seven Z-Types as the canonical starting taxonomy; treat the other files in `personas/` as supplementary texture that adds industry-standard behavioral detail within or across them.

# Athleisure shopper

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

45% buy online at least three times a year, with 22% year-over-year frequency growth. Comfort (42%) and fit (16%) rank far above price (6th) as purchase drivers — markedly less price-sensitive than general apparel shoppers.

Source: https://gearbunch.com/pages/athleisure-industry-statistics

## Commentary implications

- Lead with comfort/performance framing over discount framing — this persona is closer to [brand-loyalist.md](brand-loyalist.md) than [deal-hunter.md](deal-hunter.md) in price sensitivity, even without brand loyalty specifically.
- Fit still matters a lot (16% of purchase driver weight) — don't skip fit commentary just because price sensitivity is lower; see [../commentary/hedging-fit-claims.md](../commentary/hedging-fit-claims.md).
- Natural crossover with [../categories/clothing-shoes-accessories-sport.md](../categories/clothing-shoes-accessories-sport.md)'s Sport category, but this persona also buys athleisure styled for everyday, non-sport wear — don't assume every session is activity-specific.

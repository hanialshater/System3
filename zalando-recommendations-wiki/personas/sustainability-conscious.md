# Sustainability-conscious shopper

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

Defined by actual filter/browsing behavior (sustainability filters, resale/rental engagement) — **not** by stated preference. 76% of consumers self-identify as "conscious consumers," but only 38% actually shop that way, and income does not predict sustainable-shopping frequency or spend.

Source: https://thesustainablefashionforum.substack.com/p/76-of-people-call-themselves-conscious

Gen Z specifically wants material/supply-chain transparency (73% want sustainability transparency, 74% want origin transparency) but distrusts vague claims — 45% are actively concerned about greenwashing.

Source: https://lifestylemonitor.cottoninc.com/this-is-the-fiber-gen-z-prioritizes-while-embracing-authenticity-sustainability/

## Commentary implications

- **Don't infer this persona from a demographic guess or from a customer merely browsing a sustainability-tagged collection once.** Require an actual repeated behavioral signal (filter use, pre-owned engagement) — see the shared caution in [README.md](README.md).
- When it is warranted, ground any sustainability claim in specific, verifiable detail (fiber content, supply chain fact) — vague claims actively backfire with this persona, they don't just underperform.
- The natural home category for this persona is [../categories/outlet-lounge-preowned.md](../categories/outlet-lounge-preowned.md)'s pre-owned format.

# Capsule-wardrobe minimalist

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

Deliberately buys fewer, higher-quality items worn more often — the behavioral inverse of [trend-seeker.md](trend-seeker.md). Low purchase frequency, high per-item consideration time, resistant to trend-cycle/impulse triggers. Search interest in "capsule wardrobe" surged ~89% in a single month, suggesting a real and growing segment, not a niche one.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsule_wardrobe

## Commentary implications

- Quality/durability framing lands better here than novelty or trend framing — the opposite emphasis from [trend-seeker.md](trend-seeker.md).
- Long consideration time per item suggests this persona will actually read a detailed comparison or confidence note rather than skimming — a good candidate for [../patterns/for-comparison-friction-do-comparison-rx.md](../patterns/for-comparison-friction-do-comparison-rx.md)-style responses when comparison signals appear.

## A shared caution

Every file here names a **behavioral signal**, not a demographic guess, deliberately — persona research keeps surfacing cases where the two diverge (plus-size buyers skew younger than assumed; sustainability *behavior* is much smaller than sustainability *stated preference*). If you're tempted to infer a persona from a demographic proxy rather than an actual behavioral signal, stop and check [../process/signal-checklist.md](../process/signal-checklist.md) instead.

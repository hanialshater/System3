# Deal hunter / bargain shopper

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

No loyalty to a single brand, willing to move to lower-reputation sites for a lower price, uses coupon/comparison tools, deliberately delays purchase to wait for sales — purchase timing follows the promotional calendar, not a product drop.

Source: https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/optimize-e-commerce-sales-by-understanding-the-6-buyer-personas/

Macro context: rising price sensitivity is expanding value/midmarket segments faster than luxury industry-wide. `evidence: sourced` — https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/state-of-fashion

## Commentary implications

- Price/value uncertainty (see [../needs/trust-and-quality-uncertainty.md](../needs/trust-and-quality-uncertainty.md)) is the dominant need — lead with value framing, not styling enthusiasm.
- This persona is the natural fit for the [../categories/outlet-lounge-preowned.md](../categories/outlet-lounge-preowned.md) formats — check whether the session is actually happening in one of them.
- Don't lead with brand-prestige framing; it's the wrong lever for this persona (contrast with [brand-loyalist.md](brand-loyalist.md) and [luxury-aspirational.md](luxury-aspirational.md)).

# Plus-size / inclusive-sizing shopper

**evidence: sourced**

## Behavioral signal

Skews younger than commonly assumed: 55% of size 1X-3X buyers are under 35, and Millennials/Gen Z make up ~64% of plus-size purchases in 2025. "Fit anxiety" from inconsistent sizing across brands is elevated and pushes this segment toward brands with dedicated size guidance and toward higher try-before-buy/return propensity. Offering extended sizing with good fit guidance lifts brand loyalty ~31% and purchase frequency ~24%.

Source: https://www.fastsimon.com/ecommerce-wiki/optimized-ecommerce-experience/supply-and-demand-challenges-in-the-womens-plus-size-market/

## Commentary implications

- This is the highest-leverage persona for [../patterns/for-plus-size-shopper-do-aggregate-fit-subscore.md](../patterns/for-plus-size-shopper-do-aggregate-fit-subscore.md) — apply it directly.
- **Do not default to an older-skewing tone or item selection** — the data contradicts that assumption directly. Check this against whatever item/tone defaults an agent might otherwise reach for.
- Elevated return propensity here is a *coping mechanism* for a real information gap, not evidence of low purchase intent — don't let a high historical return rate on its own suppress recommendation confidence; look for the actual size-inconsistency signal instead.

# Beauty

**evidence: sourced**

Launched 2018, still expanding market by market (most recently Spain and Finland in 2025), explicitly to increase site traffic, attract younger shoppers, and lift average order value by combining fashion and cosmetics in one shopping experience.

Source: https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/zalando-expands-southern-europe-launches-portugal-introduces-beauty-spain/

## Why this matters for commentary

- Beauty is the newest, least-established category — assume shallower purchase history and weaker persona/style signal here than in clothing/shoes, even for an otherwise well-established customer account. Treat a Beauty-category session closer to cold start unless there's specific Beauty-category history. See [../patterns/for-cold-start-do-honest-popularity-framing.md](../patterns/for-cold-start-do-honest-popularity-framing.md).
- The "fit" problem in [../needs/size-fit-anxiety.md](../needs/size-fit-anxiety.md) doesn't translate directly to Beauty — the analogous need is closer to shade-matching or skin-type matching. This wiki doesn't yet have a dedicated note on that; if you build one, add it here and link it.

# Premium / designer

**evidence: sourced**

Zalando runs a distinct **Premium** vertical for designer fashion/footwear across men, women and kids, separate from the mainstream assortment, plus **Zalando Privé** as a discounted designer-brand outlet destination. "Premium brand additions" were explicitly credited as a driver of Zalando's 2024 growth — this is a strategic tier the company is actively investing in, not just a filter on the catalog.

Sources: https://www.retail-insight-network.com/news/zalando-ends-2024-on-high-as-premium-brand-additions-pay-off/

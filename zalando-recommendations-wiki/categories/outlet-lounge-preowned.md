# Outlet, Lounge, pre-owned

**evidence: sourced**

Three off-price/alternative formats layered on the core catalog:

- **Zalando Lounge** — members-only flash-sale app, 2,500+ brands, up to 75% off, own plus-size and premium-outlet sub-sections.
- **Outlet** — physical and online off-price channel, 500+ brands, previous-season/single-size/minor-defect stock, discounted up to 70%; now also carries pre-owned items and Beauty.
- **Pre-owned** — formerly the standalone **Zircle** resale app (launched 2018), since folded into the core platform and physical Outlet stores rather than run separately.

Sources: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.zalando.lounge · https://corporate.zalando.com/en/how-our-outlets-work · https://ecommercenews.eu/zalando-discontinues-zircle/

## Why this matters for commentary

- A customer here is very likely matching [../personas/deal-hunter.md](../personas/deal-hunter.md) or [../personas/sustainability-conscious.md](../personas/sustainability-conscious.md) (for pre-owned specifically) — price/value framing and material-provenance framing are more load-bearing here than elsewhere.
- Stock in these formats is often single-size or limited-run — a fit claim needs even more caution than usual, since the item itself may not restock for a fit-confidence signal to accumulate against. See [../commentary/hedging-fit-claims.md](../commentary/hedging-fit-claims.md).
- Marketplace/Partner Program items can carry thinner data history than first-party or established private-label items — see [../needs/trust-and-quality-uncertainty.md](../needs/trust-and-quality-uncertainty.md).

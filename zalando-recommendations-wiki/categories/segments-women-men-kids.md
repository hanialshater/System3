# Segments: Women, Men, Kids

**evidence: sourced**

Zalando's site architecture splits first by shopper segment, then by product type within each.

- **Women** is the largest segment, roughly 340,000 items, led by accessories/bags (~24%), shoes (~14%), and tops (~11%).
- **Men** carries roughly 190,000 items, led by t-shirts/polos (~19%), bags/accessories (~18%), and pants (~14%).
- **Kids** is anchored by the in-house **Friboo** private-label brand alongside third-party children's lines, spanning the same clothing/shoes/accessories structure as the adult segments.

Source: https://www.yocabe.com/en/which-products-to-sell-on-zalando-analysis-of-the-main-categories-of-items/

## Why this matters for commentary

- Accessories/bags dominate the Women's assortment by item count — don't assume "clothing" is the default mental model for what's being recommended.
- Kids commentary has a different cadence entirely — see [../personas/occasion-gift-and-family-shopper.md](../personas/occasion-gift-and-family-shopper.md) on the growth-cycle-driven replenishment pattern specific to parent shoppers.

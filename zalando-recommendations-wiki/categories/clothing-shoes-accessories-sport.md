# Clothing, shoes, accessories, sport

**evidence: sourced**

The main product-type groups within each segment (see [segments-women-men-kids.md](segments-women-men-kids.md)).

## Sport is not a flat category

Sport is organized into activity-specific "Worlds of Sport" hubs (running, football, basketball, …), each with curated gear, inspiration, and advice — an editorial/curated layer on top of the plain product taxonomy, extending into equipment and accessories, not just apparel.

Source: https://corporate.zalando.com/en/fashion/going-extra-mile-zalando-inspires-customers-stay-active-sports-category

## Why this matters for commentary

- A sport-category customer is often arriving with an activity-specific mission (training for a run, starting a new sport) rather than a general style browse — this is closer to [../needs/discovery-vs-targeted-search.md](../needs/discovery-vs-targeted-search.md)'s "targeted mission" end than its "exploratory" end, and pairs naturally with [../personas/athleisure-shopper.md](../personas/athleisure-shopper.md) for the crossover, comfort-driven segment.
- Because Sport already has an editorial/curated layer, commentary here can lean on activity-specific framing ("good for long runs") more directly than in categories without that structure.

## The underlying attribute layer

The catalog runs on a **Smart Product Platform** managing millions of products and thousands of structured attributes (1.8M AI-enriched product-detail-page attributes as of 2025), plus ontology-based modeling of which attributes are complementary vs. mutually exclusive (certain colors, fits, or style tags can't co-occur). This means the taxonomy an agent works against is substantially machine-augmented, not hand-tagged — expect noise and gaps, not a clean ground truth.

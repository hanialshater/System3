## 0. The job in one paragraph

Write Chapter 11 as the first place in the book where the author's own running institution carries the architecture, and where that architecture faces a person outside it. Open with the product: a reader on a page guesses, then sees why. Go behind the minute to the company of agents that made it, show at most three mechanisms at work on that one artefact, report what the design did **not** predict, put the objective and its owner on the table, and end on the fact that no real learner has been asked. ## 4. Mandatory honesty

Each of these is a sentence or clause **in the chapter**, beside the claim it limits, in the book's fence register ("That sentence needs a fence around it.", `02:211`), not a disclaimer section.

1. **One model in many roles is one witness.** Every catch between agents (a fact-checker disputing a builder, a reviewer narrowing a request, a COE finding against its author) is one copy of a model catching another (record header; C1-C14 limit lines). Say it the first time a catch appears (§2), and wherever a catch is used as evidence afterwards, in a clause, not a repeated paragraph. The only witnesses outside the model are the founder, code that runs (gates, tests, the replay), and third-party system records (hosting statuses, CI run states, the live site). Never call an agent's check "independent" without that qualifier.
2. **No real learners.** 0 of 1,283 lessons carry human-learner evidence; the real-learner test is designed, not run (C7). No sentence may imply a lesson teaches, works for people, or has users, unless the founder supplies a real session.
3. **The book's planted-false-diagnosis test has not been run** (Note on Evidence, "A Test Still to Run"; the company's EXP-001 adapts it and is designed, not run, A9). If §6 uses the planted-bug run (C5), say in the same paragraph that it tests whether a test suite can fail, not whether a false belief survives into the next worker's plan. No incident in the chapter is presented as a result of that test.
4. **Production versus the release log (C8), if used.** C8 is the record's own find and contradicts the company's release log and one COE. If the draft uses it: only after the fact-checker has re-run the checks with fresh timestamps; framed as "the log ran ahead of production and a third-party record settled it"; with the company's own flags ("not re-confirmed") credited, since nothing was concealed. No production URL.
5. **Evidence category in the prose.** Use the book's words, not the record's labels in brackets: "seen once, and the commits are still there to reread" (observed, re-checkable); "a script reruns it" (run); "written down; nobody has measured whether it works" (designed); "the company's own count, which I have not recomputed" (testimony-grade). 6. **No composites in company material.** Nothing combined, changed, embellished or moved in time. If an illustrative scene is wanted, it is labelled illustrative in the sentence and is not evidence. 7. **Every number dated "as of 17 October 2026"**, once per cluster (not per number), and rest the argument on events and mechanisms rather than totals (SS R6). ## 5. Voice, headings, references

### 5.1 Voice (BS §6; `.claude/skills/ai-tells/SKILL.md`)

- **Baseline: Chapters 4 and 5.** Argument comes out of a scene; self-deprecation that carries a point; one joke per idea, often a deflating clause at the end of a serious sentence; code or a field as punchline (`"humanLearners": []` is the chapter's candidate; use it once, in §8, or once in §1 and once in §8 at most); honest fences in-line; closing question hands to the next chapter.
- **First person.** The book is in the author's first person. You may write first-person *argument* consistent with positions the book already holds ("I would want to see…"). You may **not** write first-person *experience*: what he saw, felt, decided, typed, worried about, or did on a given day. Where a recorded act of the founder's is needed (he approved a deploy with a known risk; he set a 10,000-lesson target), state it as recorded fact, add no motive or feeling, and put an AUTHOR comment beside it asking him to confirm or voice it.
- **Scenes from the record, not invented.** The company has real events; use them as they are recorded, with no added dialogue, time of day, weather, screens or reactions.
- **Agents are not people.** Use roles in plain words ("the coordinator", "the fact-checker", "the release manager"). **No persona names** (the company's and the press's agents have human first names; seed risk with Chapter 13's names, and R8 register).
- **Generalise vendors and services:** "the hosting service" (its free plan's 100 deployments a day may stay as a number), "the code host", "the CI system", "a database service", "another company's coding agent", "one vendor's model". No product, repository, URL, region or model names, unless the founder accepts naming (section 8, D5). The company itself is named, "System 3 Company", as the commission's subject (D5 default).
- **Founder's words to the company: not quoted verbatim**, except at most **three** places, each with an adjacent AUTHOR comment asking permission and giving the paraphrase that replaces it if he declines. Candidates (record §G; quote exactly, including spelling, or not at all): "Ship it anyway" (C9, §7); "What is going on you are working all the day but number of demos did not move" (C6, §5); "yes, deploy r7" (C1, §5). Format:
  `<!-- AUTHOR: May the book quote your words to the company here, exactly as typed: "…"? If not, the sentence becomes: "…". (Commission objection 4.) -->`
- **Checker targets** (`python3 resources/editorial/check-tells.py 11 --lines`, calibrated on Chs 4-5): **maxim% ≤ 30, notXbutY/1k ≤ 0.5, cite-chains 0, xref/1k ≤ 0.5.** The old chapter met all four (21%, 0.4, 0, 0.0); match it. Negative parallelism ("not X but Y", "X is not Y. It is Z") was this chapter's dominant tell in earlier passes and is the one most likely to return; a company chapter will also invite **cross-chapter stitching** (the company implements Chs 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10) and **coinage inflation** (gates, board, rituals, constitution, COE).
- **Specific rules:** no chapter numbers in running text (xref target), at most one decodable callback per section, glossed for a cold reader; **no new bold term** (the de-bolding request for Chs 9-11 stands); **zero em-dashes** (the old chapter had none); colons sparingly; none of the skill's vocabulary list (delve, landscape, robust, seamless, leverage, journey, unlock, harness…); no "This chapter…" signposting; no closing recap.

# About the Author

Hani M.M. Al-Shater has died twice.

The first time, he was a kid, and someone explained that “superhero” was not a job. Inventor seemed worth a try. A computer would at least let him make things nobody had asked for. The second time, years into a respectable career, he caught himself looking forward to the next turn of the promotion wheel. He had wanted to make things. When had he started wanting another turn?

These days he lives in Berlin with his wife and four children and serves as Head of Applied Science at Zalando. He still wants to know what an idea is good for, which causes trouble in philosophical company, and whether it was a good idea in the first place, which can hold up an engineering meeting.

At midnight he makes a coffee too hot to drink and takes his questions to an AI in incognito mode. By morning their conversation has vanished. It is an odd habit for a man who wrote a book about keeping records.

# NotebookLM videos for System 3

Prepared 20 September 2026 from the local manuscripts, including the accepted Chapter 5 edits. Each numbered chapter has a short steering prompt and a detailed storyboard. The preface has an optional standalone opening prompt. No manuscript prose was changed.

## Use a chapter pack

1. Add that chapter’s manuscript, including its notes, and its matching storyboard to the notebook as sources. Select those sources for the adaptation.
2. Paste the matching `video-prompt.txt` into the video customization instructions. Use English. The short prompt carries the sequence; the selected storyboard preserves detailed scenes, visual mappings and qualifications.
3. Review the generated video against the storyboard, especially its reveals, numeric claims and distinction between experiments and proposed work. Shot order, pacing and exact delivery are requests, not guaranteed controls.

Use the chapter’s current title, not the older title suggested by its filename. The source manuscript governs facts and wording. These packs adapt the book; they are not independent fact-checks. Avoid selecting the entire book when that would pull later arguments into an earlier chapter’s reveal.

Chapter 5 retains the original detailed brief, updated to use the accepted seven ingredient labels and their explicit compiler mapping. Chapter 13 asks for a short fictional story with protected twists. Chapter 14 contains only the two closing sentences and asks for a coda, not a padded explainer. The preface’s time-sensitive Navier–Stokes report is conditional on supporting primary-source material being selected.

# Chapter 7 opener — candidate review and decision

Five candidates reviewed 2026-08-30 against `resources/art-direction/README.md`
and the chapter's conceptual turn (the machinery of improvement becomes
editable while running; self-reference ≠ self-improvement; "better" must live
outside the editable surface).

- **1-open-chest-robot** — right world, wrong idea: literal title, labeled
  "IMPROVEMENT TEST" checklist (banned explanatory prop), no possibility of
  failure, invented subtitle. Salvage: strip the checklist and it becomes an
  interior vignette candidate.
- **2-nine-rings-grid** — generic recursion wallpaper; off-palette. Keep one
  germ: the telescope aimed at itself (#3).
- **3-ten-concepts-sheet** — best concept vocabulary, wrong world (dark glossy
  concept art; the android head is the banned genre). Concepts worth keeping:
  The Editor, The Time of Improvement (hourglass), The Incomplete Circle.
- **4-train-bridge** — **CHOSEN.** The only candidate where improvement happens
  in motion, with human witnesses and real stakes. Fixes required: replace the
  sci-fi glow with pencil-construction-lines-completing-themselves; add one
  collapsed earlier span in the mist (the descendant can be worse); add a small
  trackside signal post on the old side (the harness the new track still
  passes).
- **5-brass-mirror** — conceptually dense, maximally illegal: five labeled
  props, six loud objects. Steal wordlessly: the locked vault outside the
  mirror's reach → became the signal post in the chosen direction.

Final brief lives in `book-design/manifests/chapter-07.json` (`opener_brief`)
and exports to `book-design/art-prompts/chapter-07.md`. Subtitle is the
manuscript's: *When Science Turns Inward*.

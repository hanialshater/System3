# Style Experiment — Slavic accent (vyaz lettering + Bilibin frames)

Purpose: judge a possible inflection of the book's visual language before
committing to it. Same scenes as the current accent, two changes only:
display lettering in vyaz construction logic (Latin alphabet, English text),
and Bilibin-style ornamental border frames around scenes. Everything else —
palette, watercolor+ink, cream paper, storybook robots — held constant.

Compare outputs against `../art-candidates/ch07/4-train-bridge.png` (current
accent). Decision criteria: does the accent add identity without adding
folklore, and does the vyaz band stay readable at thumbnail size?

Hard rules for both jobs:
- Latin alphabet, English text. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: faux-Cyrillic letter
  substitution (no Я for R, И for N, Д, Ш, etc.). Slavic lettering
  *construction*, normal Latin letters.
- No onion domes, firebirds, kokoshniks, or Russian-folklore props.
- Palette unchanged: muted Prussian/denim blue, ochre, warm gray, olive,
  parchment cream. No gold leaf, no saturated color.
- No readable text beyond specified title lines; no labels inside scenes.

## Job 1: opener test  →  `../art-experiments/slavic/ch07-opener.png`

2:3 portrait (1600×2400px). Early-20th-century Slavic book illustration
manner (Ivan Bilibin): flat restrained watercolor, strong fine ink contours,
warm cream paper with grain.

Title area (upper ~25%), hand-lettered: small blue letterspaced "CHAPTER 7";
large black title "Recursive Self-Improvement" lettered in vyaz construction
logic — tall condensed verticals, letters sharing strokes, woven into one
dense band; blue handwritten subtitle "When Science Turns Inward".

Scene (lower ~75%): old iron-and-stone railway bridge over a deep gorge at
warm dusk; a steam train mid-crossing whose forward half — and the span
ahead — are still pencil construction lines on bare paper, inking and
watercoloring themselves as the train advances (no glow, no neon: the future
is an unfinished drawing completing itself). Far below in mist, one earlier
collapsed span. On the old stone side, a small trackside signal post. Two
ordinary human figures watch from the cliff edge, backs to us.

Frame: Bilibin-style ornamental rectangular border band around the scene
(not the title) — flat geometric pattern strip in muted Prussian blue and
ochre, 2–4% of page width, hand-drawn imperfection welcome.

## Job 2: interior plate test  →  `../art-experiments/slavic/ch07-gradebook.png`

Rectangular plate around 1400×1000px, same Bilibin manner, enclosed in the
same style of ornamental border band.

Scene: an empty classroom at dusk; at the teacher's desk a small hand-built
storybook robot has the gradebook open, pen hovering — its own row of marks
already suspiciously perfect. Through the window, unfinished homework blows
across the yard. No readable text anywhere (the gradebook shows only
illegible marks).

## If the vyaz band comes out illegible

Iterate with: "20% less weaving; letter identity always wins over ornament;
widen the three narrowest letters." Do not abandon the lettering style on
the first failure — vyaz density is tuneable.

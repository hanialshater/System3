## Five Ways This Could Be Wrong

I have claimed that as we build autonomous AI, we keep rediscovering science as its architecture. Here is how I would attack that claim if somebody else had made it.

**It is only an analogy.** Any group of fallible workers needs records and review. Courts have them. So does an accounting department. Why science and not law? Because of what the agents produce. A court settles a dispute by authority, and a market settles it by price. The agents in these chapters produce claims: the patch works, the metric means what we think, the proof checks. The institution whose product is a claim that can be revised is science. Where the question turns to who decides, the book does reach for law. Chapter 7 needed a constitution, and Chapter 9 needed a boundary the evidence cannot cross. Science for what is known. A constitution for who decides.

**I found what I was looking for.** I had read Popper before I read the traces, and the agents learned from human text, so of course they rebuild human institutions. Two things count against this. Carlini was building a compiler, and his records and tests came from repairs. And my own epistemic agent in Chapter 4 solved fewer problems; if I was arranging the evidence, I arranged it badly. What would count against the thesis is a system doing open-ended work reliably, at scale, with no provenance, no independent check and no preserved disagreement. Chapter 2 marks the boundary: when the referee is cheap and exact, almost none of this is needed. The thesis concerns work where checking is expensive or ambiguous.

**Science is shaped by human limits.** Careers, journals, tenure, funerals. Agents have none of them. True, and Chapter 6 says so: session turnover is not Planck's funeral. What transfers is whatever answers fallibility and coordination: a claim with an address, a test committed before the result, an objection with a consequence. Whatever answers mortality and ambition does not have to come along. A swarm should not automatically become a meeting.

**Science has no customer.** A research community can follow a question anywhere. A product serves someone. Yes, which is why the architecture is more than science. Layer 4 stays outside it. The institution can investigate what a choice would do. It cannot appoint whose purposes prevail.

**“Ultimate” is a big word.** I have no uniqueness theorem. Science replaced earlier ways of knowing and may be replaced itself. The claim I can defend is smaller and stranger: this is the architecture that contains procedures for replacing its own parts, its judge included. If something better arrives, it can arrive as an amendment.

None of this shows that the composition works. I have shown pieces. Chapter 6 said which.

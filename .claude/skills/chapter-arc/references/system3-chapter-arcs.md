## B. Chapter arc

### B1. The five strong chapters

**Ch4, System 3** (4,621 words, 10 sections, 8 citations)

- **Opening:** a participatory puzzle. A photo of the author with a camel and seven claims, with the promise "The answers are at the end" (`04:5-21`).
- **Running cases:** the camel (bookends), the tongue-ear test (`:25-37`), the farmer and the cow (`:39-61`), Alberto (`:73-87`), and epistemic-swe (`:202-277`).
- **Section order:** it moves outward from the shortest trust chain: body (Shortest Trust Chain), then language (Saussure), testimony (Alberto), development (Face), the name (System 3), action (Code Can Touch Back), memory (Hallucination With Better Retention), experiment (Epistemic Agent), the counter-move (Creative Distrust), and the return (Back to the Camel).
- **Sources:** each enters through an image. Saussure through the word "cow" (`:49-55`), Wittgenstein through "fire" (`:63`), Bender–Koller's octopus (`:65`), and Kahneman as the setup for the coinage (`:127`).
- **Lived experience:** his mother's face (`:93-101`), a sibling's bullshit (`:97`), eight years ranking testimony with "It's Tuesday" (`:196-200`), and his own experiment.
- **Coined term:** *System 3*, "If System 1 proposes and System 2 deliberates, System 3 checks" (`:131`), with the lighter mnemonic Gut/Head/Hand (`:133`).
- **Turn:** the author's experiment goes against him. The epistemic agent solved fewer problems (`:222`), the headline number "flatters it" (`:231`), and the 13579 failure shows accumulated trust steering the agent wrong (`:267-277`). That leads into Creative Distrust.
- **Ending:** the camel answers as a callback (`:295-303`), then a widening question (`:315-317`).

**Ch5, The Society of Agents** (7,761 words, 9 sections, 23 citations)

- **Running cases:** the compiler, bookended (`:283-305`), and the potter/apprentice (`:92`, `:138`, `:158`).

### B2. The reusable chapter template

1. **Title plus italic subtitle.** The subtitle is often witty or explanatory (`04:3`, `05:3`, `07:3`).
2. **Open on a concrete thing, never a thesis.** The options seen so far: a puzzle the reader takes part in (Ch4), a joke-line plus a reported case (Ch5), an ordinary person's small scene (Ch7), a fresh incident (Ch8), or a callback plus an everyday micro-case (Ch9). Within a page the author steps in personally (`04:9`, `05:14`, `08:15`, `09:11`). The problem is posed as a question or a single loaded word (`07:21`).
3. **One running case, threaded and bookended,** usually with a second, smaller human thread (potter, Omar, Mallorca). Sections return to the case to show what each new idea changes about it.
4. **Sections are named as moves or images, not topics.** They are imperatives (Ch8), rungs (Ch7), functions (Ch5) or steps outward (Ch4). There are typically 7–10 `##` sections and rarely `###`. Section titles are vivid phrases ("The Student Finds the Gradebook", "Boyle's Pump"), and a section often ends on a one-line pivot into the next.
5. **Sources come through scenes, one source per point.** A named person, a date and a physical act (Ibn al-Haytham darkening a room, `05:186`). The source is then turned back onto the agent case ("The agent version is almost embarrassingly literal", `05:204`). Philosophers are introduced as tools, not surveyed.
6. **Lived experience shows up at two or three load-bearing points.** Usually one early aside, one mid-chapter "this is personal" paragraph (`04:196`, `05:130`) and, ideally, the author's own experiment or failure. The skill names these scenes as the source of the baseline chapters' strength (`.claude/skills/development-edit/SKILL.md:29-32`).
7. **One coined term,** introduced once around the middle or late in the chapter, after the case has earned it (`04:131`, `07:188`, `08:113`, `09:41`). It is bolded only if it is a definition.
8. **A turn that goes against the author or the easy reading.** His own experiment loses (Ch4). The organization is the bug (Ch5). Freezing the evaluator fails (Ch7). The safety tool removes safety (Ch8). The same trip installs a desire (Ch9). Evidential limits are stated next to the claim (`04:214`, `:231`, `:235`; `08:105`).
9. **End with a callback, then an image or question.** Close the opening case (camel answers, sixteen Claudes again, Omar's dog). Then either reverse the frame (`05:303-305`, `08:119`) or ask the question the next chapter will answer (`04:315`, `09:121`). No summary.
10. **Length:** 3,000–7,800 words (median about 4,600), 7–10 sections, with citations used densely but never as a list.

### B3. Where the weaker chapters break the template

**Ch1** (2,899 words, 0 citations, 4 AUTHOR markers)

- **Opening:** it opens on a general riff about life and pineapple (`01:8-18`), not a scene.
- **Running case:** none. The case it has is imagined and flagged as such: the agent run (`:44-46`), the AlphaGo placeholder (`:54`) and the jacket (`:82-84`).
- **Coined terms:** it announces the framework as four bolded slogans ("Complexity over engineering", "emergence over design", "capacity over power" and the bet, `:30`, `:48`, `:104`, `:112`), against the house rule that ideas are discovered, not announced (`prompts/chapter-version-evaluation.md:28`).
- **Sources and lived experience:** no sources enter through scenes, and the lived experience stays abstract (`:26`, `:96-102`).
- **What works:** the ending (`:116-118`).

**Ch10** (2,306 words, 1 citation)

- **Running case:** it has a real one, the author's editing request (`10:5-31`), and the strongest second-coffee-test pages.
- **Middle sections:** Bureaucracy on the Fly, Selective Friction, and Invisible by Default (`:43-67`) are one-to-three-paragraph slogan sections, close to "consulting deck" headings. The readiness evaluation counts 13 bold spans in the slogan-heavy middle (`resources/evaluations/2026-09-30-chapter-readiness-evaluation.md`, Ch10 row).
- **Turn:** it moves out of the case into a separate objections essay (`:87-109`). The essay is strong but book-level, not chapter-level.
- **Sources:** almost none (Sutton, `:95`).

**Ch11** (4,785 words, 14 sections, the most of any chapter, 1 citation)

- **Opening and running case:** the lived frame, "I built a prototype store" (`11:5`), never produces a lived moment. The AUTHOR note says so (`:7`), and the customers are imagined (`:9`).
- **Register:** it reads like a design document, with "not X, it is Y" closers ("System 3 is no longer a chapter about hallucinations. It is a product requirement", `:121`; "The customer is not the funnel", `:55`).
- **Coined term:** a bolded *problem fingerprint* (`:49`), alongside many other named concepts.
- **Turn:** the turn ("The Book Comes Back to Bite Me", `:237-245`) is a self-aware coda, not a complication inside the case.

**Ch12** (5,824 words, 9 sections plus 2 subsections)

- **Opening and ending:** strong. Dantzig (`12:5`) is closed by callback (`:271`).
- **Running case:** none through the middle. The chapter hops from the curve to the river, bespoke tools, "anyone could do that", Derrida, Ostrom, the laboratory and the second descent.
- **Lived experience:** it is concentrated in reflection; the community and the irrigators are imagined (`:143`, `:261`).
- **Coined terms:** several compete: double descent, the ideology vortex (`:101`) and capacity over power.
- **Turn:** distributed across sections, though the "Tuesday room" (`:153-159`) is a template-grade micro-turn.

The common failure is the absence of a lived, threaded case plus coinages that are announced rather than earned. The evidence note confirms it: Ch1 is "Argued", Ch11's experiment "has not run", and Ch12 is "Argued… no new experiment" (`appendix-note-on-evidence.md:7,18,19`).

## C. The author's rubric

The rubric is stated at `.claude/skills/development-edit/SKILL.md:17-27`, with Chapters 4–5 as the calibration baseline (`:29-32`). The house style protections are at `prompts/chapter-version-evaluation.md:26-35`.

**Summary.** The strongest chapters earn every rubric point through one mechanism: a lived or vividly reported case threaded through argument-move sections, with sources entering through scenes, one honest turn against the author, and a callback ending that hands off a question. The weaker chapters fall short on ownership (lived scenes), not on line quality. That matches the skill's warning that "line editing alone tops out at around 7.5" (`SKILL.md:62-64`).
